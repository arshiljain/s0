Cookies on the ICO website

We use some essential cookies to make this site work. We also set analytics cookies to understand how you use this site. We may use services from Vimeo and YouTube that may also use cookies.

For more detailed information, see our Cookies page(Opens in a new window)

Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for core functionality, such as security and network management. They always need to be on.

Analytics cookies

We use Silktide to measure how you use the ICO website. These cookies collect information about how you got to the site, the pages you visit and how long you spend on each page, and what you click on.

Video player cookies

Some pages of the ICO website include videos hosted on Vimeo and YouTube. If you enable this setting, this may result in the video sharing platform collecting information about your viewing for analytics and advertising purposes. If you don’t enable this setting, the pages will include external links instead.

Make a complaint

Need help? Start a live chat or call our helpline on 0303 123 1113.

Our normal opening hours are Monday to Friday between 9am and 5pm (excluding bank holidays).

The Information Commissioner is not in a position to respond in person to all the matters raised with their office by the public. The Commissioner delegates such casework correspondence to be dealt with by their staff.

Data protection complaints

Including problems accessing your information, the way your data has been handled, other people's data and internet search results.

Nuisance calls and messages

If you have received unwanted electronic marketing via telephone, email or text, find out who to contact.

FOI and EIR complaints

If you've a problem with a freedom of information, environmental information or a re-use request, make a complaint or find out what to do next.

Cookies

If you're concerned about the use of cookies on a website, you can report your complaints to us.

Whistleblowing

Tell us about wrongdoing, risk or malpractice related to data protection or information rights that you have witnessed at work.

Complaints and compliments about us

If you are dissatisfied with our service, or the way we have handled your personal information you can complain. You can also let us know if you think there is something we have done well.

Home CCTV complaints

If you feel a home CCTV user isn’t following data protection law – find out what to do next.

UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and US Government entities complaints

Make a complaint about the handling of your personal information by a US business registered under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or the unlawful access of your personal information by US Government entities (including the US Intelligence Community) after it has been transferred from the UK to a US organisation using any transfer mechanisms.
